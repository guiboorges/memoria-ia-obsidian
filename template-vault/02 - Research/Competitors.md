# Competitors

| Type | Strength | Weakness | What to borrow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large gym management systems | Full-featured | Expensive and complex for a small studio | Automatic waitlist |
| Generic schedulers | Cheap | Don't understand classes or packs | An easy-to-share booking link |
| WhatsApp + spreadsheet (what the studios use today) | Everyone already knows how to use it | Lots of manual work, no-shows go unchecked | The friendly tone of the messages |

**Conclusion:** there's room for something simple, built for small studios. See the principles in [[MASTER-CONTEXT]].
