# Booking flow

Three screens, less than 10 seconds (principle 1 in [[MASTER-CONTEXT]]).

1. **Schedule** — list of the studio's upcoming sessions, with open spots.
2. **Confirm** — session, time, price or pack credit. One button: "Book".
3. **Done** — confirmation, an "Add to calendar" button and a link to cancel.

If the session is full, the button becomes "Join the waitlist" (see [[Glossary]]).
